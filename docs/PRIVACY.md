# Privacy Notice (plain language)

*This notice is written for the young people who use I Matter, in simple
words. A copy of the same promise appears inside the app (About → Your
privacy, and My Space).*

## What we promise

- **No account. No sign-up. No password to any company.** You just open
  the app and use it.
- **We do not ask for your name.** The app never needs your full legal
  name, phone number, or address.
- **Nothing you write leaves your device.** Your journal, goals,
  strengths, and everything in My Space is saved only on the phone,
  tablet, or computer you are using. It is not uploaded, shared, or sent
  anywhere — the app doesn't even need the internet.
- **No advertising. No tracking.** There are no ads, no analytics, no
  cookies for tracking, and no social media buttons.
- **You are in control.** You can delete everything at any time:
  *My Space → Delete all my data*. You can also add a PIN so My Space is
  protected on your device.

## What the app stores (on your device only)

- Which lessons you explored and badges you earned
- Anything you write in My Space
- Your settings (language, text size, contrast)

## Things to know

- **Shared devices:** if you use someone else's phone, remember your
  entries stay on THAT phone. Use *Delete all my data* when you finish, or
  avoid writing private things on shared devices.
- **Your progress** (lessons reviewed, badges) is counted only on your own
  device and is never shown to anyone else or sent anywhere.
- The app works completely offline once installed. It only needs the internet
  the first time it is installed, and afterwards to check for app updates from
  the website it was installed from.

## For parents, guardians, and schools

I Matter is intentionally designed for data minimization: no personal data
is collected, transmitted, or stored on any server. All user-generated
content lives in the browser's local storage on the device and can be
erased by the user in one tap. Questions can be directed to Imagine
Tomorrow Foundation using the contact details in the app's About section.
